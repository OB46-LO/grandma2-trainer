    Nachschlagen

    Alle Tasten erklärt

    PC-Tastatur im Simulator: Ziffern, Punkt, + und −, Enter = Please, Esc = Esc, Rücktaste = Oops, F = Fixture, G = Group, T = Thru, A = At, C = Cue, P = Preset, S = Store.
